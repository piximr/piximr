Discipline Overview

Personal Art

Watercolour studies, fine digital illustrations, and experimental artistic explorations rooted in emotion and texture.

Artistic Expression

Traditional sensibility meets digital freedom.

Beyond commercial deliverables, Piximr explores visual fine art as an essential research laboratory. We investigate how the fluidity of traditional watercolours and hand-drawn strokes can inform digital compositions.

Each artwork explores organic palettes, loose brush textures, and evocative light scenarios, maintaining the human touch in an increasingly algorithmic digital age.
